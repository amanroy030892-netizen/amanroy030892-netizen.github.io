Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

SafarSaathi Community Platform

1. Introduction

SafarSaathi connects passengers directly with local E-Rickshaw drivers and carpool owners. We operate on a 100% direct passenger-to-driver payment model with ₹0 commission on ride fares.

2. Privacy & Data Security

  • Masked In-App Voice Calls: Agora RTC handles private real-time calls without exposing telephone numbers.
  • Aadhaar KYC Verification: Document photos and QR information are strictly utilized for identity authentication.
  • Real-time Location: Driver location is broadcast on radar only during active 12-hour shifts when set to On Duty.

3. Platform Fee & Zero Commission

  • Drivers pay a flat ₹100 platform fee for 12 hours of live radar access.
  • Passengers pay 100% of ride fares directly to drivers via personal UPI/Cash.

4. Cancellation Policy

Carpool reservations may be cancelled up to 15 minutes prior to scheduled departure. Helpline support is available via WhatsApp: +91 6901027507.
